Shop groceries with your Florida EBT card on Amazon — free delivery over $35, no Prime required, and a discounted Prime option if you want it.

Updated June 20, 2026 • 8 min read

Why Amazon EBT Is Underrated

SNAP recipients across Florida often don't realize Amazon accepts EBT directly. For rural Florida (Panhandle, North Central, Southwest counties) where the nearest grocery store may be 30 miles away, Amazon delivers SNAP-eligible foods straight to your door at no extra cost on $35+ orders.

For urban Floridians (Miami-Dade, Broward, Orange, Hillsborough), Amazon offers a way to skip the trip and have heavier items (rice, beans, canned goods, frozen meat) delivered.

Step-by-Step: Link Florida EBT to Amazon

  1. Go to amazon.com/snap-ebt.
  2. Click "Get Started". Sign in or create a free Amazon account.
  3. Enter your 16-digit Florida EBT card number (printed on the front of your blue/yellow Florida EBT card).
  4. Enter your EBT PIN. Amazon does NOT store this — you re-enter it at every checkout.
  5. Add your Florida shipping address (must be a real residential address — no PO boxes for grocery delivery).
  6. Browse the SNAP-eligible items by going to amazon.com/snap-eligible. Items will show a green "SNAP EBT eligible" tag.

FAQs

Can I use my Florida EBT card on Amazon?

Yes. Amazon accepts SNAP EBT in all 50 states including every Florida ZIP code. You can buy SNAP-eligible groceries with EBT and pay other items (delivery fees, taxes, non-food items) with a separate card.

Do I need Amazon Prime to use EBT on Amazon?

No. SNAP recipients get free delivery on orders over $35 without Prime. If you want Prime benefits, EBT/Medicaid recipients also qualify for a discounted Prime membership ($6.99/month).

What can I buy on Amazon with Florida EBT?

SNAP-eligible foods: fresh fruits and vegetables, meat, poultry, fish, dairy, breads, cereals, snack foods, non-alcoholic beverages, seeds and plants that produce food. You cannot buy alcohol, tobacco, hot prepared foods, vitamins, supplements, household supplies, or pet food with EBT.

How do I link my Florida EBT card to Amazon?

Go to amazon.com/snap-ebt. Click "Get Started." Enter your EBT card number, then your PIN. Amazon does not store your PIN — it is only used to verify at checkout.

Are there other Florida stores that accept EBT online?

Yes — Walmart, Aldi, Target, Whole Foods (via Amazon), Publix (in some areas), Sams Club, and Instacart through Aldi/Costco. All major grocery chains in Florida now accept SNAP online.
