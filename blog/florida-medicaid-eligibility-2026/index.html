Florida Medicaid Eligibility 2026: Who Qualifies, Income Limits & How to Apply

Medicaid is Florida's largest health insurance program, covering over 5 million residents. But eligibility varies dramatically by category. Here's exactly who qualifies in 2026.

Quick answer: Florida Medicaid covers children up to 211% FPL (under age 1), 145% (ages 1–5) and 138% (ages 6–18), with KidCare covering children up to 215% FPL; pregnant women up to 196% FPL; aged/disabled adults up to 88% FPL; and parents only up to 26% FPL (KFF, January 2026). Due to Florida's refusal to expand Medicaid, non-pregnant adults without children have very limited access.

Florida Medicaid Eligibility by Category (2026)

👶 Children (0–18)
Income limit: Up to 215% FPL via Medicaid + KidCare ($70,950/year for family of 4)
Full Medicaid — doctor visits, hospital, dental, vision, mental health, prescriptions
🤰 Pregnant Women
Income limit: Up to 196% FPL (about $53,547/year for a household of 3)
Prenatal care, delivery, and 12 months postpartum coverage
👴 Elderly (65+) & Disabled
Income limit: Up to 88% FPL (~$14,045/year for 1 person)
Full Medicaid including long-term care. May also qualify for Medicare Savings Programs.
👪 Parents & Caretaker Relatives
Income limit: Up to ~26% FPL (~$8,580/year for family of 4) — extremely restrictive
Full Medicaid benefits if income is low enough
🚫 Non-Pregnant Adults Without Children
Income limit: No Medicaid (except for emergency-only care)
Florida has NOT expanded Medicaid. About 800,000 adults fall in the "coverage gap" — too poor for ACA subsidies, not eligible for Medicaid.

Florida's Coverage Gap — 800,000 Adults Stuck

Florida is one of 10 states that has not expanded Medicaid under the Affordable Care Act. This creates a "coverage gap" where adults who earn too little for ACA Marketplace subsidies (under 100% FPL) are also ineligible for Medicaid (limit is just 26% FPL for parents, zero for childless adults).

If you're in this gap, your options include:

  • Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs) — sliding-scale fees based on income. Call 1-800-525-8282 or visit findahealthcenter.hrsa.gov
  • GoodRx / Mark Cuban's Cost Plus Drugs — for prescription assistance
  • Hospital charity care — most Florida hospitals have financial assistance programs
  • Community health partnerships — many counties have free or sliding-fee clinics

How to Apply for Florida Medicaid in 2026

Online (Fastest)

Apply at ACCESS Florida: myaccessflorida.com. Create an account and submit your application online. You'll receive a determination letter within 45 days (7 days for emergency Medicaid).

By Phone

Call 1-866-762-2237 Monday–Friday 7am–6pm EST. A customer service representative can help complete your application or check your status.

By Mail or Fax

Download and complete Florida Form DCF-628. Mail to your local DCF office or fax to 1-866-886-4342.

Florida Medicaid 2026 FAQ

Is Florida Medicaid the same as Medicaid in other states?

Florida administers its own Medicaid program called "Florida Medicaid." While it follows federal rules, Florida chooses which optional populations to cover and which services to include. Florida notably does NOT cover non-pregnant adults without children (no Medicaid expansion).

How long does Florida Medicaid approval take?

Standard applications: within 45 days. Applications involving disability: within 90 days. Emergency Medicaid (for those with urgent medical need): within 7 days. Pregnant women: expedited processing is available — call 1-866-762-2237.

Can I keep Medicaid if I start working?

If your income increases above the limit, you'll receive a notice before benefits end. Children have a 12-month continuous coverage period. Adults may have a short grace period. If you lose Medicaid due to income, you may qualify for subsidized coverage through Healthcare.gov.
