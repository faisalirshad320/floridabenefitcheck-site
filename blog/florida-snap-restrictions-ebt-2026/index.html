Florida SNAP Restrictions 2026: Complete Banned & Allowed EBT List

Florida received federal approval to restrict certain SNAP purchases starting April 2026. Here is the complete, official list of what is now banned and what remains allowed on Florida EBT cards.

📅 April 1, 2026•⏱️ 8 min read•Source: USDA FNS, Florida DCF

Important: These restrictions took effect April 20, 2026 in Florida. Attempting to purchase banned items with your EBT card will result in a declined transaction at checkout.

What Changed in Florida SNAP April 2026?

Florida DCF, under Governor Ron DeSantis, requested a federal waiver in May 2025 from the USDA to restrict SNAP purchases of candy, soda, and energy drinks. The USDA approved the waiver, making Florida one of a small number of states with approved SNAP purchase restrictions.

🚫 NOW BANNED in Florida (2026)

  • ✗Candy and candy bars
  • ✗Soda and carbonated soft drinks
  • ✗Energy drinks (Red Bull, Monster, etc.)
  • ✗Sports drinks (Gatorade, Powerade) with added sugar
  • ✗Artificially sweetened candies
  • ✗Tropical punch drinks with less than 50% juice

✅ STILL ALLOWED on EBT

  • ✓Fruits and vegetables (fresh, frozen, canned)
  • ✓Meat, poultry, and fish
  • ✓Dairy products (milk, cheese, yogurt)
  • ✓Bread, cereal, rice, and pasta
  • ✓100% fruit juice (no added sugar)
  • ✓Water and sparkling water
  • ✓Baby formula and baby food
  • ✓Seeds and plants to grow food
  • ✓Amazon grocery delivery (with EBT)

What Remains Permanently Banned (Unchanged)

These items were always prohibited and remain so in 2026:

  • ✗Alcohol and alcoholic beverages
  • ✗Tobacco products and cigarettes
  • ✗Hot prepared foods
  • ✗Vitamins and medications
  • ✗Household supplies (soap, paper products)
  • ✗Pet food
  • ✗Non-food items

How to Use Amazon EBT in Florida

Despite the new restrictions on candy and soda, Florida EBT cardholders can still use their benefits on Amazon.com for grocery delivery. Amazon's system automatically filters out restricted items at checkout when EBT is selected.
