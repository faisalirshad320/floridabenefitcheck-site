About FloridaBenefitCheck.com

FloridaBenefitCheck.com is a free, independent benefits eligibility checker built specifically for Florida residents. Our mission is to help the millions of Floridians who qualify for government assistance programs but don't know it, find and access the help they're entitled to.

Our Mission

Only 71% of eligible Floridians receive SNAP benefits. Hundreds of thousands of eligible people are not enrolled in Medicaid or SSI because the application process is confusing, the eligibility rules are complex, and no single free tool existed to check multiple programs at once — in multiple languages. We built that tool.

Privacy & Data

We collect zero personal information. All eligibility calculations happen entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is transmitted to our servers or stored anywhere. We do not require signup, account creation, or email address to use the checker.
