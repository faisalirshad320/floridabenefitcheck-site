TANF Florida 2026
Temporary Cash Assistance

Florida's cash assistance program for families with children in deep poverty. One of the strictest in the U.S. — only 38% FPL.

⚠️ TANF Florida — Strict Requirements

  • ! 48-month lifetime limit across all states. Once you hit it, you can't get TANF again.
  • ! Work requirement: 30+ hours/week of work or job training, or 20 hrs/week if you have a child under 6.
  • ! Must have minor children in your home (under 18, or under 19 if a full-time high school student).
  • ! Income limit ~38% FPL — Florida is one of the strictest states.
  • ! Asset limit: $2,000 in countable resources.
