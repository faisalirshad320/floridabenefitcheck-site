👶Nutrition Program

WIC Florida 2026
Women, Infants & Children

Free healthy food, baby formula, breastfeeding support, and nutrition counseling for pregnant women, new mothers, and children under 5. Serving over 500,000 Floridians monthly.

Check WIC Eligibility →Florida WIC Website

Who Can Get WIC in Florida?

🤰

Pregnant Women

During and up to 6 weeks after pregnancy (or end of pregnancy). Includes all trimesters.

🤱

Breastfeeding Women

Up to 12 months after the baby's birth if breastfeeding. Receive enhanced food package.

👩

Postpartum Women

Non-breastfeeding women up to 6 months after delivery or end of pregnancy.

👶

Infants

Babies from birth through 11 months. Formula, baby food, and iron-rich cereal provided.

🧒

Children Under 5

From 1 year old through the month they turn 5. Nutritious foods plus health screenings.

What Foods Does WIC Provide?

Food Benefits (WIC Card / EBT-WIC)

  • ✓ Milk, cheese, yogurt
  • ✓ Eggs
  • ✓ Whole grains (bread, cereal, oats)
  • ✓ Fruits and vegetables ($26-$52/month cash value benefit)
  • ✓ Baby formula (infants only)
  • ✓ Baby food fruits and vegetables
  • ✓ Iron-fortified infant cereal
  • ✓ 100% fruit/vegetable juice
  • ✓ Canned/dried beans, peanut butter
  • ✓ Canned fish (tuna, sardines, salmon)

Additional WIC Services

  • 🏥 Health screenings — height, weight, hemoglobin, blood lead levels
  • 🍼 Breastfeeding support, education, and peer counselors
  • 📋 Nutrition education and counseling
  • 🔗 Referrals to Medicaid, SNAP, and other programs
  • 📍 Local WIC clinic appointments (or video/telehealth options)

How to Apply for WIC in Florida

1

Call Your Local WIC Clinic

WIC is run through Florida County Health Departments. Call 1-800-342-3556 or visit the Florida WIC website to find the clinic nearest you.

Find WIC Clinic →
2

Schedule an Appointment

You'll need to come in for an initial certification appointment. Bring your proof of identity, residency, income, and pregnancy/medical documentation.

Call 1-800-342-3556 →
3

Attend Your Certification

At your appointment, a WIC staff member will verify your eligibility, check your nutrition status, and issue your WIC benefits card (Florida uses an EBT-style WIC card).

Documents to Bring to Your WIC Appointment

□ Photo ID (driver's license, passport, state ID)
□ Proof of Florida address (utility bill, lease, etc.)
□ Proof of income (pay stubs, tax return, benefit award letter)
□ Proof of pregnancy (doctor's note or prenatal records)
□ Child's birth certificate (for children)
□ Immunization records (for children)
□ Child's Medicaid card (if applicable)
□ Baby's pediatrician contact info
