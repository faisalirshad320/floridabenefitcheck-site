Who Qualifies for LIHEAP in Florida?

✓
Income Eligible
Gross household income at or below 150% of the Federal Poverty Level (see table above)
✓
Florida Resident
You must live in Florida and be responsible for your home energy costs
✓
US Citizen or Qualified Alien
Documentation of immigration status required for non-citizens
✓
Priority Given To
Elderly (60+), disabled individuals, households with children under 5, and those facing shutoff

Auto-qualify: If you already receive SNAP, SSI, TANF, or SSP benefits, you may automatically qualify for LIHEAP without an income check.

How to Apply for LIHEAP in Florida

1

Apply Online via ACCESS Florida

The fastest way. Go to ACCESS Florida and create an account or log in to apply for LIHEAP assistance.

Apply at ACCESS Florida →
2

Contact Your Local Community Action Agency

LIHEAP is administered locally by Community Action Agencies. Call 211 to find your local agency and ask about current funding availability.

Call 211 →
3

Apply In Person at DCF

Visit a Florida DCF office with all required documents. Call ahead to confirm LIHEAP funds are currently available in your county.

Find DCF Office →

⚠️ Important: LIHEAP Funds Are Limited

  • ! LIHEAP is funded through federal block grants with limited annual funding. Once funds run out, you must wait for the next program year.
  • ! Application periods open and close throughout the year. Apply as early as possible when the program opens.
  • ! Crisis assistance for utility shutoffs may have funds available year-round in some counties. Call 211 for urgent help.
  • ! Payment goes directly to your utility company — you won't receive cash.
