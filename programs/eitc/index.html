💰Federal Tax Credit

EITC Florida 2026
Earned Income Tax Credit

Up to $8,231 refund for working Florida families. 1 in 5 Floridians eligible miss this credit. Free tax filing available.

2026 EITC Income Limits & Max Refund

Your maximum federal EITC depends on filing status and number of qualifying children. Florida has no state income tax, so this is your only tax benefit at the state level — claim it!

Qualifying ChildrenMax CreditSingle Income LimitMarried Filing Jointly
0$664$19,540$26,820
1$4,427$51,593$58,863
2$7,316$58,629$65,899
3+$8,231$62,974$70,244

Investment income limit: $12,200. Must have valid Social Security Numbers for you, your spouse (if filing jointly), and all qualifying children.

Who Counts as a Qualifying Child?

A child qualifies if they meet ALL of these tests:

  • 👶 Relationship: Son, daughter, stepchild, foster child, sibling, half/step-sibling, or descendant of any of these.
  • 📅 Age: Under 19 at year-end, OR under 24 if full-time student, OR any age if permanently disabled.
  • 🏠 Residency: Lived with you in the U.S. for more than half the year.
  • 📋 Joint return: Cannot file a joint tax return (except to claim refund).
  • 🆔 SSN: Must have a valid Social Security Number.

💡 Bonus: Child Tax Credit (CTC)

EITC stacks with the Child Tax Credit. For 2026, the CTC is $2,200 per qualifying child under 17, of which up to $1,700 is refundable as the Additional Child Tax Credit (ACTC).

A working Florida family with 2 children could realistically receive $7,316 EITC + $4,400 CTC = $11,716+ in tax refunds.

⏰ Don't Miss the Deadline

Tax filing deadline: April 15, 2027 (for 2026 income year).

Even if you don't owe taxes, you must file to receive EITC. You have 3 years to file a late return and still claim EITC. Stop missing money you're owed.
