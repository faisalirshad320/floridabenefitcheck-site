SNAP

Food Stamps / EBT
Max benefit
Up to $808/mo (family of 3, from Oct 1, 2026)
Income limit
200% FPL gross income (Florida)
Who qualifies
Anyone with low income — 3M+ Floridians on it
Read full guide →
🏥

Medicaid

Health Coverage
Max benefit
Free or low-cost health insurance
Income limit
Children 200% FPL, adults 26% FPL
Who qualifies
Children, pregnant women, elderly, disabled, very-low-income adults
Read full guide →
♿

SSI

Supplemental Security Income
Max benefit
Up to $994/mo (2026 federal SSI)
Income limit
$994/mo individual; assets ≤ $2,000
Who qualifies
Elderly (65+), blind, or disabled with low income
Read full guide →
⚡

LIHEAP

Energy Bill Assistance
Max benefit
Up to $700 cooling / $700 heating / $1,000 crisis
Income limit
150% FPL
Who qualifies
Households struggling with utility bills
Read full guide →
👶

WIC

Nutrition for Mothers & Kids
Max benefit
~$50/mo of food + formula + services
Income limit
185% FPL
Who qualifies
Pregnant women, new mothers, infants, kids under 5
Read full guide →
💵

TANF

Cash Assistance
Max benefit
Up to $303/mo (family of 3)
Income limit
38% FPL — Florida is very strict
Who qualifies
Families with dependent children in deep poverty
Read full guide →

Florida KidCare

Children's Health Insurance
Max benefit
$0–$20/mo per family for full coverage
Income limit
≤200% FPL subsidized; full price above 200%
Who qualifies
Uninsured children up to age 18
Read full guide →

Lifeline

Phone & Internet Discount
Max benefit
$9.25/mo off phone or internet
Income limit
135% FPL or on SNAP/Medicaid/SSI
Who qualifies
Low-income individuals needing connectivity

EITC

Earned Income Tax Credit
Max benefit
Up to $8,231 refund (3+ children)
Income limit
Working income up to ~$70,244
Who qualifies
Working families & low-income workers
