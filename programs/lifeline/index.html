Lifeline Florida 2026
Phone & Internet Discount

Federal program providing $9.25/month off phone or broadband service for low-income Florida households. Up to $34.25/mo if you live on tribal lands.

Check Lifeline Eligibility →Apply at Lifeline Support

Who Qualifies for Lifeline?

You qualify if EITHER your income OR your enrollment in another assistance program meets the rules:

✓ By Income

Household gross income at or below 135% of Federal Poverty Level:

  • • 1 person: $1,796/mo ($21,546/yr)
  • • 2 people: $2,435/mo ($29,214/yr)
  • • 3 people: $3,074/mo ($36,882/yr)
  • • 4 people: $3,713/mo ($44,550/yr)
  • • Each additional: +$639/mo

✓ By Program Participation

Auto-qualify if you participate in:

  • • SNAP / Food Stamps
  • • Medicaid
  • • SSI (Supplemental Security Income)
  • • Federal Public Housing Assistance
  • • Veterans Pension or Survivors Benefit
  • • Certain Tribal programs (Bureau of Indian Affairs)

Important Rules

  • • One per household. Only one Lifeline benefit per household (not per person).
  • • Annual recertification. You must re-verify eligibility each year.
  • • Discount on ONE service only: phone OR broadband — not both at the same time.
  • • Cannot transfer to another household member.
